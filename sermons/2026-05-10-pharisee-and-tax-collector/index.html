Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
The Pharisee and the Tax Collector 法利賽人與稅吏
(Luke 路加福音 18:9-14)

Intro: Stories Jesus told — Luke 18:9-14引言：耶穌所講的比喻——路加福音 18 章 9-14 節
Today's service date: 10th May 2026. We come to another of the stories Jesus told — The Pharisee and the Tax Collector (Luke 18:9-14). It is a short parable, but it cuts straight to the heart of how we see ourselves, how we see other people, and how we come to God. 今天是 2026 年 5 月 10 日主日崇拜。我們再來看耶穌所講的另一個故事——法利賽人與稅吏（路加福音 18 章 9-14 節）。這個比喻雖然短小，卻直指我們的內心——我們如何看自己、如何看別人、又如何來到神面前。
………………………………………………
1. This v that — comparing things一、這個與那個——比較
Which is better — sunshine or rain? A book or a games console? Cabbage or chocolate? 哪一樣比較好——陽光還是下雨？書本還是遊戲機？椰菜還是朱古力？
We are always comparing things. And often we end up comparing people too. 我們常常都在比較事物。而且很多時候，我們也會比較人。
Today's parable will show us that this kind of comparing — comparing ourselves with others — is both silly and dangerous. 今天的比喻會讓我們看見：這樣比較——把自己跟別人比較——既愚昧，也危險。
………………………………………………
2. Us v them — the two characters二、我們與他們——兩個主角
Who are the two characters in today's story? A Pharisee and a Tax Collector. 今天故事裡有哪兩個主角？一個法利賽人和一個稅吏。
A Pharisee was part of a small but elite group — Bible teachers and religious leaders, respected and respectable. If you were a dad, this is the kind of man you would want your daughter to marry. If you were a homeowner, this is the kind of man you would want as a neighbour. Good, law-abiding, looked up to. 法利賽人是一個小群體，但屬於精英階層——他們是聖經的教師、宗教領袖，受人尊敬，行為端正。若你是父親，你會希望女兒嫁這樣的人；若你是業主，你會希望這樣的人做你鄰居。他們循規蹈矩、受人景仰。
A Tax Collector, on the other hand, was working for the Roman occupiers — a traitor in his own people's eyes. He ripped people off, was mean, greedy, heartless. Like a corrupt police officer working for the wrong side, only ever caring about himself. 稅吏則相反——他為羅馬殖民者做事，在同胞眼中是叛徒。他剝削人民、為人刻薄、貪婪、冷酷無情，就像一個收受賄賂、為敵方做事的貪污警察，只顧自己的利益。
We know what others thought of these two — but Jesus also tells us what each of them thought of himself. 我們知道別人怎樣看這兩個人——但耶穌還告訴我們，他們各自又如何看自己。
Both went to the temple to pray. Notice in v.11 and v.13 that both stood by themselves — but for very different reasons. 兩個人都到聖殿裡禱告。請留意第 11 節和第 13 節，兩人都是獨自站著——但原因卻完全不同。
The Pharisee stood apart because he felt superior. His prayer began: "God, I thank you that I am not like other people…" And in v.12 he tells God how he fasts twice a week — far more than the law required. 法利賽人獨自站著，是因為他覺得自己高人一等。他禱告開頭就說：「神啊，我感謝你，我不像別人……」然後在第 12 節，他向神細數自己一週禁食兩次——遠超過律法的要求。
The Tax Collector stood apart because he felt awful about himself. He looked down at the ground. He didn't compare himself to anyone — he simply said: "God, have mercy on me, the sinner." Literally, "the sinner" (v.13) — as if he were the only one. 稅吏獨自站著，是因為他對自己充滿羞愧。他低著頭看地上。他沒有跟任何人比較——他只是說：「神啊，開恩可憐我這個罪人。」原文直譯是「那個罪人」（第 13 節）——彷彿世上只有他一個罪人。
Question: Who are we more like? 問題：我們較像哪一位？
Look at v.9 — Jesus tells this parable to those who "were confident of their own righteousness and looked down on everyone else." Are we like that? Smug about how we're doing — and looking down on others? 請看第 9 節——耶穌講這比喻，是說給那些「仗著自己是義人，藐視別人」的人聽。我們是否像這樣？對自己的表現沾沾自喜，又看不起別人？
Why is that a problem? 這為甚麼是個問題？
1. It is ugly. Jesus hates pride, arrogance, and self-righteousness. They make us ugly in his eyes. 一、這是醜陋的。耶穌恨惡驕傲、自高自大、自以為義。在祂眼中，這些都使我們變得醜陋。
2. It is naïve. It is not deep enough. It is all about the outside — how we look, how we behave. The outside does matter — how we treat parents, teachers, fellow students. But there is more to it. The outside might be excellent, while the inside is full of pride, prejudice, contempt and even hatred. 二、這是膚淺的。它的層面不夠深。它只看外在——我們的外貌、我們的行為。外在當然重要——例如我們如何對待父母、老師、同學。但事情並不止於此。外在或許表現極佳，但內裡卻充滿驕傲、偏見、輕視，甚至仇恨。
3. It misunderstands the gospel. The Kingdom of God is the realm of grace and mercy, not achievement and performance. To rely on our own righteousness is to miss the whole point of how Jesus saves. 三、這誤解了福音。神的國是恩典與憐憫的國度，不是靠成就與表現的國度。靠自己的義來到神面前，正是錯失了耶穌救贖的核心。
………………………………………………
3. Just Jesus!三、單單仰望耶穌！
Be like the Tax Collector — not the other guy. 要像那稅吏——不要像那位法利賽人。
Read v.14: "I tell you that this man, rather than the other, went home justified before God. For all those who exalt themselves will be humbled, and those who humble themselves will be exalted." 請讀第 14 節：「我告訴你們，這人回家去，比那人倒算為義了；因為凡自高的，必降為卑；自卑的，必升為高。」
There is one thing that can keep us out of the Kingdom of God: thinking we earned our way in. When it becomes all about us and our performance — that shuts the door. 有一件事會讓我們進不了神的國：以為我們是憑自己賺到位置的。當焦點變成自己和自己的表現——天國的門就關上了。
Only one thing is really needed — come to Jesus with empty hands and say: "Lord, have mercy." That's all Jesus needs. And it is all we can give. 其實只需要一件事——以空空的雙手來到耶穌面前，說：「主啊，求祢憐憫我。」這就是耶穌所需要的，這也是我們所能獻上的。
Question: What does Jesus say about that man in v.14? — "Justified." 問題：耶穌在第 14 節怎樣形容那個人？——「算為義」。
What does that mean? It means 100% right with God. The big lesson is this: only Jesus has the right to judge. 這是甚麼意思？意思是在神面前完完全全得稱為義。這個比喻的大道理是：只有耶穌有資格作審判。
That is wonderful for two reasons: 這事為何美好？有兩個原因：
1. If you come to Jesus saying "Lord, have mercy" — with repentance and faith — and you don't move from that posture, Jesus answers: "YES! You are mine, just as you are." 一、若你來到耶穌面前說「主啊，求祢憐憫我」——以悔改和信心而來——並一直不離開這個位置，耶穌就回答你：「是！你是屬於我的，就照你現在的樣子。」
2. Only Jesus' opinion really matters. You may feel inadequate; you may not compare well with others — who cares! Jesus loves you, and he always will. 二、只有耶穌的看法才真正重要。你或會覺得自己不夠好；與別人相比可能總是落後——那又如何！耶穌愛你，並且永遠愛你。
………………………………………………
Closing thought 結語結語
So today, where do you stand? Like the Pharisee — comparing, congratulating yourself, looking down — or like the Tax Collector, with empty hands and an honest heart, saying: "God, have mercy on me, the sinner"? 所以今天你站在哪一邊？像法利賽人那樣——比較、自我吹噓、輕看別人——還是像稅吏那樣，以空空的雙手、誠實的心說：「神啊，開恩可憐我這個罪人」？
That second prayer is all that Jesus needs — and that is exactly the prayer he loves to answer. 這後一個禱告，正是耶穌所需要的——也正是祂最樂意回應的禱告。

📖 Summary 摘要
English

Jesus tells this short parable to people who were "confident of their own righteousness and looked down on everyone else" (Luke 18:9). Two men go up to the temple to pray. One is a Pharisee — religious, respectable, the kind of person everyone admires from the outside. The other is a Tax Collector — a traitor, despised by his own people, the kind no one would invite home.


What surprises us is not how others see them, but how each man sees himself. The Pharisee thanks God that he is not like everyone else, listing his religious achievements. The Tax Collector cannot even lift his eyes; he simply prays, "God, have mercy on me, the sinner." Jesus' verdict is shocking: it is the Tax Collector — not the religious man — who goes home justified, declared right with God.


The point is sharp: comparing ourselves with others is both silly and dangerous. Self-righteousness is ugly, naïve, and ultimately blocks us from grasping the gospel. The Kingdom of God is the realm of grace, not performance. The only prayer Jesus needs from us is the prayer of empty hands: "Lord, have mercy." And to that prayer, again and again, Jesus says yes.

繁體中文

耶穌講這個簡短的比喻，是對那些「仗著自己是義人，藐視別人」的人說的（路加福音 18 章 9 節）。兩個人上聖殿去禱告：一個是法利賽人——敬虔、體面、人人從外面看都會稱讚的那種人；另一個是稅吏——是叛徒、被同胞唾棄、沒有人會請他到家中作客的人。


令人意外的，不是別人怎樣看他們，而是他們各自怎樣看自己。法利賽人感謝神自己不像別人，又一一細數自己的宗教成就。稅吏卻連抬頭也不敢，他只是禱告說：「神啊，開恩可憐我這個罪人。」耶穌作出的判決令人震驚：竟是稅吏——而不是那虔誠的人——回家時得稱為義，在神面前算為公義。


這個比喻的重點直指人心：把自己與別人比較，既愚昧也危險。自以為義是醜陋的、是膚淺的，最終會攔阻我們明白福音。神的國是恩典的國度，不是表現的國度。耶穌唯一需要我們獻上的禱告，就是空空雙手的禱告：「主啊，求祢憐憫我。」對於這樣的禱告，耶穌一次又一次地回答：「是。」

💡 Key Themes 主要主題
1. The danger of comparing 比較的危險
English
We are always comparing — sunshine or rain, this person or that. But when we apply that habit to people, we end up either smug or crushed. Jesus shows that this comparing-instinct is both silly and spiritually dangerous; it leaves us blind to ourselves and unkind to others.
繁體中文
我們常常都在比較——陽光還是下雨、這個人還是那個人。但當我們把這種習慣加在人身上時，結果若不是自以為高人一等，就是被擊倒灰心。耶穌讓我們看見，這種愛比較的本性，既是愚昧的，也是屬靈上危險的——它使我們對自己一無所見，又對人毫無溫柔。
2. Outside vs inside 外在與內裡
English
The Pharisee's outside was excellent — fasting, tithing, religious observance. None of that was wrong in itself. The problem was the inside: pride, prejudice, contempt, the looking-down-on-others. God sees both, and a polished outside cannot hide a hard heart.
繁體中文
法利賽人的外在表現非常出色——禁食、十一奉獻、敬虔的宗教生活，這些本身並沒有錯。問題出在他的內裡：驕傲、偏見、輕視別人。神看見的是兩面——亮麗的外表，並不能遮蓋一顆剛硬的心。
3. Self-righteousness misunderstands the gospel 自以為義是誤解了福音
English
The Kingdom of God is the realm of grace and mercy — not achievement and performance. To stand before God on the strength of our own record is to miss the whole point of how Jesus saves. The gospel is for those who come empty-handed.
繁體中文
神的國是恩典和憐憫的國度——不是靠成就和表現的國度。若我們倚靠自己的功績站在神面前，就完全錯失了耶穌救贖的核心。福音是為那些空空雙手而來的人預備的。
4. The prayer Jesus loves 耶穌所喜悅的禱告
English
"God, have mercy on me, the sinner." This short prayer, spoken with empty hands and an honest heart, is the only prayer Jesus needs from us. It is also the only prayer we are truly able to give. To it, Jesus says, again and again, "Yes."
繁體中文
「神啊，開恩可憐我這個罪人。」這句簡短的禱告，以空空的雙手和誠實的心獻上，正是耶穌所需要我們獻上的唯一禱告，也是我們真正能獻上的唯一禱告。對這樣的禱告，耶穌一次又一次地回答：「是。」
5. Justified — 100% right with God 「算為義」——在神面前完完全全得稱為義
English
Jesus says the Tax Collector went home justified. That means 100% right with God — not because of anything he did, but because he came in humble faith. Only Jesus has the right to judge, and his verdict on the humble heart is always: "You are mine."
繁體中文
耶穌說稅吏回家時被稱為義。意思是在神面前完完全全得稱為義——這不是因他做了甚麼，而是因他以謙卑的信心來到神面前。只有耶穌有資格作審判，祂對謙卑之心的判決永遠是：「你是屬於我的。」

🌱 Application 生活應用
English

This week, try the Tax Collector's prayer. First thing in the morning, last thing at night, simply: "Lord, have mercy on me, the sinner." Don't dress it up. Don't list achievements. Just come empty-handed.


Notice your comparing. When you catch yourself measuring up against someone — better than them, or worse — pause and bring that moment to Jesus. Replace the comparison with thanksgiving (for them) and honesty (about yourself).


Care less about reputation, more about reality. Where are you spending energy looking good rather than being honest? Pick one place — home, workplace, social media — and choose, just this week, to be more honest than impressive.


Receive Jesus' verdict. If you have come to Jesus saying "have mercy" and have not moved from that posture, his answer is "You are mine, just as you are." Let that be the voice that names you this week — louder than any other voice.

繁體中文

這個禮拜，試一試稅吏的禱告。每天早上起來第一件事、晚上臨睡前最後一件事，簡簡單單地說：「主啊，開恩可憐我這個罪人。」不要修飾它，不要羅列成就，只以空空的雙手前來。


留意自己的比較。當你發現自己又在跟別人較量——感覺自己比他好，或不如他——請停下來，把那一刻帶到耶穌面前。用感恩（為對方感恩）和誠實（對自己誠實）來代替比較。


少在乎名譽，多在乎真實。你最近在哪些地方花了很多力氣讓自己看起來不錯，而不是真實？挑一個範疇——家裡、職場、社交媒體——這個禮拜選擇活得「真實」過於「亮眼」。


接受耶穌對你的宣告。若你已經來到耶穌面前，說「求祢憐憫我」，並一直不離開這個位置，祂的回答就是：「你是屬於我的，就照你現在的樣子。」這個禮拜，讓這個聲音成為定義你的聲音——比任何其他聲音都響亮。

🙏 Prayer 禱告
English

Father in heaven, thank you for the simple, shocking grace of this parable. We confess that we are quick to compare, quick to congratulate ourselves, slow to look honestly at our own hearts. Forgive us where pride has hardened us — where we have measured ourselves by others and missed your verdict altogether.


Lord Jesus, teach us to pray with empty hands. Today, with the Tax Collector, we say: "God, have mercy on me, the sinner." We come not with our achievements but with our need. Let your blood-bought verdict — justified — quiet our restless hearts. Help us to receive it daily, and to live this week in the freedom of being yours.


Holy Spirit, grow in us a humble spirit that is gentle with others, honest about itself, and confident in Christ. May the prayer of the Tax Collector become the heartbeat of our whole lives — until we see Jesus face to face. In his name we pray, Amen.

繁體中文

天父，感謝祢藉著這個簡短卻震撼人心的比喻，向我們顯明祢的恩典。我們承認自己很容易比較、很容易自我吹噓，卻不願誠實地省察自己的內心。求祢赦免我們因驕傲而剛硬的心——我們以別人作量度的標準，反而完全錯失了祢的判決。


主耶穌啊，求祢教導我們以空空的雙手禱告。今天我們與稅吏一同說：「神啊，開恩可憐我這個罪人。」我們來到祢面前，不是憑著我們的成就，而是憑著我們的需要。願祢用寶血所換來的判決——「算為義」——平靜我們不安的心。求祢幫助我們每天領受這份恩典，這個禮拜活在屬於祢的自由裡。


聖靈啊，求祢在我們心中培養一份謙卑的靈：對人溫柔、對己誠實、在基督裡有把握。願稅吏的禱告，成為我們一生跳動的心音——直到有一天，我們得以面對面見祢。奉耶穌的名禱告，阿們。
